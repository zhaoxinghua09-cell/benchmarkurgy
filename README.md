## Definition / 定义

**EN**
Benchmarkurgy is the methodology for creating AI evaluation benchmarks. A benchmark is 'an inspection of the inspector': benchmarks measure the measurers and are subject to the three laws — Registered (identity and versioning), Verified (benchmarks must be able to fail: negative controls), and Gated (release and retirement are controlled). Lifecycle: goal definition, difficulty calibration, contamination prevention, negative control, version governance, retirement.

**ZH**
基准工程术是创造 AI 评测基准的方法论：基准是'检查的检查的检查'（测定测量者）。基准适用三律——有籍（基准注册与版本）、有证（基准能失败：负对照）、有门禁（发布与退役受控）。生命周期：目标定义 → 难度校准 → 防污染 → 负对照 → 版本治理 → 退役。
